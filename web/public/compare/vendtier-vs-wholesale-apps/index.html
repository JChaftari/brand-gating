Vendtier vs. all-in-one wholesale apps: which do you need?

By the Vendtier team · November 2026

The Shopify App Store has plenty of wholesale apps. Most are all-in-one B2B suites: registration forms, wholesale price lists, quantity breaks, payment terms and more. Vendtier is deliberately narrower. It does one thing: each customer's discount on each brand, applied automatically at checkout.

That makes it a great fit for some stores and the wrong tool for others. Here's how to tell which you are.

What Vendtier does

  • A percentage per brand for each customer, set on their customer page in Shopify admin.
  • Discounts by customer tag (Premium): everyone tagged, say, gold-dealer gets the same per-brand rates, and a customer's own rate still wins for that brand.
  • Brands defined by collection or product tag, so it fits how you already organize products.
  • Applied by a Shopify discount function in the standard checkout and on draft orders. No theme code, no discount codes, works on every Shopify plan.
  • Combines with your order and shipping discounts.

What Vendtier doesn't do

If you need any of these, an all-in-one wholesale app, or Shopify's own B2B features if your plan includes them, is likely a better fit:

  • Wholesale registration and approval forms for new trade customers.
  • Showing wholesale prices on product pages before checkout.
  • Quantity breaks or volume pricing ("10% off 12+ units").
  • Net payment terms, invoicing or minimum order rules.
  • Quick-order forms or bulk ordering.

Choose Vendtier if…

  • You sell several brands to trade customers and their rates differ by brand.
  • You already know who your wholesale customers are, and they sign in to order.
  • You're tired of discount codes per customer or fixing draft order prices by hand.
  • You want something you can set up today, without changing your theme.

Choose an all-in-one app if…

  • You need trade sign-up forms, quantity breaks or payment terms.
  • Your customers must see their wholesale price on the product page before they add to cart.
  • Your customers get the same rate on every brand, so per-brand rates don't matter to you.
Using both? Vendtier's discount is a product discount. Shopify doesn't stack two product discounts on the same item, so if another app also discounts products, decide which one handles brand rates.
