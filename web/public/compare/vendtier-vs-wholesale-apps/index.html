Vendtier vs. all-in-one wholesale apps for Shopify

Vendtier does one job: each trade customer's own discount on each brand you sell, applied at Shopify checkout. All-in-one wholesale apps cover more ground. This page shows what the narrower focus gets you.

What Vendtier is built for

Stores that sell several brands to dealers, salons or stockists usually agree terms brand by brand: 25% on one brand, 15% on another, because each brand's margin differs. Vendtier stores exactly that, one number per customer per brand.

  • Per-brand rates are the core feature. Each customer's percentages sit on their own page in Shopify admin, on the Brand Discounts card.
  • Groups by customer tag. On Premium, a tag such as gold-dealer gives everyone the same brand rates, and a customer's own rate still wins for that brand.
  • Shopify's own checkout. The discount is a Shopify discount function, so it works on every plan, on draft orders, and without any theme code.
  • Five minutes to set up. Turn on brand discounts, add your brands, type a percentage on a customer. That's the whole setup.
  • A free plan that stays free. Two brands and unlimited customers at $0; Premium is $19.99 a month for unlimited brands and tiers.

Where Vendtier stops

Vendtier sets discounts and nothing else. It has no trade sign-up forms, doesn't show wholesale prices on product pages before checkout, and has no quantity breaks, minimum orders or payment terms. Customers need to be signed in at checkout so Shopify knows who they are.

Running it next to another app

Vendtier's discount is a product discount. Shopify doesn't stack two product discounts on the same item, so if another app also discounts products, let Vendtier handle the brand rates and keep the other app's product discounts off those items. Order and shipping discounts combine with Vendtier as usual.
