How to set a wholesale discount per brand on Shopify

Your trade customers rarely get the same terms on every brand. This guide sets up a separate percentage per customer and per brand with Vendtier, applied at checkout with nothing for the customer to type. It takes about five minutes, and the Free plan covers two brands.

1. Group products by brand

Vendtier needs to know which products belong to which brand. If you already have a collection per brand, or your products carry the brand in their Vendor field or tags, you are most of the way there. Vendtier creates a collection and a tag for each brand you add, and a product counts toward that brand when it is in the collection or carries the tag.

Tip: an automated collection with the rule "Vendor is equal to Corvane" keeps itself up to date as you add products.

2. Turn on brand discounts

  1. Add Vendtier from the Shopify App Store. It opens inside your Shopify admin.
  2. On the Brands page, click Turn on brand discounts. This creates one automatic discount in your store that Vendtier keeps up to date.

There is nothing to add to your theme. The discount runs in Shopify's own checkout as a discount function, on every Shopify plan.

3. Add your brands

Click Add brand and type the brand's name. Repeat for each brand you sell wholesale. On the Free plan the first two brands get discounts at checkout; Premium removes the limit.

4. Set each customer's percentages

  1. In Shopify admin, open Customers and pick a trade customer.
  2. Add the Brand Discounts card to the page the first time (the app shows you where).
  3. Choose a brand, type the percentage and save. Add as many brands as that customer buys.

When their terms change, edit the number on the same card. The next checkout uses the new rate.

5. Give a whole tag one tier

If many dealers share the same terms, you don't have to set them one by one. On Premium, create a tier for a Shopify customer tag, such as gold-dealer, and give it a percentage per brand. Every customer with that tag gets those discounts.

6. Check it on a draft order

  1. Go to Orders › Create order and pick the customer.
  2. Add a product from one of their brands.
  3. Under Payment, click Add discount, tick Apply all eligible automatic discounts and click Done.

The line drops by that customer's percentage for the brand: a $100.00 product at 20% shows $80.00. The same thing happens when the customer signs in and checks out on your store.

How the discounts combine

  • A customer's own rate wins. A percentage set on the customer's page overrides their tier for that brand. Tiers fill in the brands you haven't set.
  • Several tiers, best rate. A customer with two tagged tiers gets the higher percentage for each brand.
  • Other discounts. Vendtier combines with your order and shipping discounts. Like any product discount, it doesn't stack with a second product discount on the same item.
  • Signed in. Customers need to be signed in at checkout so Shopify knows who they are. Draft orders you create for them always apply.

Why not discount codes

Shopify's discount codes can be limited to one customer and one collection, so per-brand terms are possible with codes. The catch is the count: one code per customer per brand, so twenty dealers on five brands is up to a hundred codes to create, send and update. Customers also have to remember to enter them.

Shopify's B2B price lists, where your plan includes them, adjust a whole catalog by one percentage. Different rates per brand mean fixed prices product by product, or a separate catalog per brand. Vendtier keeps one number per customer per brand, on the customer's own page.
