Privacy Policy

Vendtier: Wholesale by Brand (the “App”) · Effective 26 October 2026

This policy explains what information the App processes when a merchant installs it on their Shopify store, why, and how it is protected. The App is provided by Chaftari Development (“we”, “us”).

What the App does

The App lets a merchant register the brands they sell and give each of their customers a discount percentage on each brand. Shopify applies those discounts automatically at checkout.

Information the App processes

  • Brand information: the brands a merchant registers — brand name, brand tag, the collection created for the brand, and an optional image link.
  • Customer discount assignments: for each customer the merchant chooses, the brand discount percentages assigned to them, stored against that customer’s Shopify record. The App does not access customers’ names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses.
  • Discount settings: the list of registered brands, stored on the store’s brand discount so Shopify can apply it at checkout.
  • Checkout: at checkout, Shopify runs the App’s discount logic inside Shopify’s own platform, using the cart’s products and the customer’s assigned percentages. This happens within Shopify; we do not receive or store cart contents.
  • Privacy requests: Shopify sends the App notifications when a merchant or customer makes a data access or deletion request. These can include a customer’s ID, email address or phone number. The App verifies and acknowledges them and does not store their contents; it records only the type of request and the store’s domain.

How we use it

Only to provide the App’s features: showing and managing brands and customer discounts in the merchant’s Shopify admin, and applying those discounts at checkout. We do not use this information for advertising, analytics, profiling or any other purpose.

Where it is stored

All App data is stored inside the merchant’s own Shopify store, in data fields that belong to the App. We do not operate a database and keep no copies of store or customer data. The App’s admin page and its privacy-request endpoint are hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request information (such as IP addresses) to deliver them, under Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information. The only services involved in running the App are Shopify (the platform the App runs on) and Cloudflare (hosting of the admin page and privacy-request endpoint).

Retention

Discount assignments exist only in the merchant’s store, and only while the merchant keeps them: they are removed when the merchant removes a customer’s discount, removes a brand, or deletes the customer. Because we keep no copies, nothing is retained on our side. If a merchant uninstalls the App, we lose access to their store; data the App stored there remains under Shopify’s control and is handled according to Shopify’s policies for uninstalled apps.

Security

  • All App data is stored by Shopify, which encrypts it at rest.
  • All communication — with Shopify and with the App’s hosted page — uses encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • The App requests only the store permissions it needs (products, customers and discounts), and privacy notifications are accepted only when Shopify’s signature verifies.

Your rights and privacy requests

Merchants decide which customers receive discounts and are responsible for their customers’ data; the App processes it on the merchant’s behalf. Customers who want to access or delete their data should contact the merchant they shop with. Shopify forwards such requests to the App, and since we hold no copies of customer data, no further data is held by us. Merchants can contact us with any privacy question using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated; significant changes will also be communicated to merchants using the App.
