Open a customer in Shopify admin and set a percentage per brand on the Brand Discounts card. Change it when their terms change; the next checkout follows. See the setup

Why no codes

Discount codes get shared, forgotten and typed wrong, and twenty dealers on five brands can mean a hundred of them. With Vendtier a signed-in customer opens checkout and every line already shows their price for its brand.

Set up Vendtier in 3 steps

Point at a step to see its screen in Shopify admin.Tap a step to see its screen in Shopify admin.

  • 1Add your brands

    Each brand gets a Shopify collection and a tag. A product counts when it sits in the collection or carries the tag.

  • 2Set each customer's discounts

    On the customer's page, type a percentage per brand on the Brand Discounts card. On Premium, give a whole tag one tier.

  • 3Let checkout apply them

    A signed-in customer sees each line's brand discount at checkout. Your theme stays as it is.

Do I need Shopify Plus?

No. Vendtier works in the standard checkout on every plan.

Does it work on draft orders?

Yes. Pick the customer, choose Add discount and tick "Apply all eligible automatic discounts".

Does it stack with my other discounts?

It combines with order and shipping discounts, but not with a second product discount on the same item.

What doesn't Vendtier do?

It sets discounts only: no sign-up forms, minimum orders or payment terms. Customers sign in to check out.

What data does it store?

Your brands and each customer's percentages, in your own store. No names, emails or addresses. Read the privacy policy
