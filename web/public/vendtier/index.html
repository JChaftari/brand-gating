How it works

Three steps, all inside your Shopify admin.

1

Register your brands

Add each brand you sell. Vendtier creates a collection for it. A product counts toward a brand when it's in that collection or carries the brand's tag.

2

Set the discounts

On any customer's page, give them a percentage for each brand. Or give everyone with a customer tag, like gold-dealer, the same brand discounts in one go.

3

They check out as usual

When a signed-in customer checks out, each item gets their discount for its brand. It also works on draft orders you create for them.

Each customer's terms, right on their page

A Brand Discounts card sits on the customer page in Shopify admin. Add a brand, type a percentage, save. Change it whenever their terms change, and checkout follows straight away.

Whole groups at once, by customer tag

Dozens of dealers on the same terms? Create a tier for a Shopify customer tag and give it a percentage per brand. Everyone with that tag gets those discounts. A discount set on one customer's page still takes priority for that brand. Premium

Applied automatically, no codes

The discount runs inside Shopify's own checkout, so nothing is added to your theme and there's nothing for customers to type. Each line shows the discount for its brand. Your order and shipping discounts still combine with it.

Questions

Do I need Shopify Plus?

No. Vendtier works on every Shopify plan. It uses Shopify's discount functions, which run in the standard checkout.

Does it change my theme or storefront?

No. Nothing is added to your theme. Signed-in customers see their brand discount at checkout.

How does Vendtier know a product's brand?

Each brand you register gets a Shopify collection and a tag. A product counts toward the brand if it's in that collection or carries the tag, so you can use whichever fits how you already organize products.

What if a customer has their own discount and is also in a tier?

Their own discount wins for that brand. Tier discounts fill in the brands you haven't set for them individually. If they're in several tiers, they get the best percentage for each brand.

Does it work with draft orders?

Yes. Create a draft order for the customer, choose Add discount and tick "Apply all eligible automatic discounts", and their brand discounts apply.

Does it combine with my other discounts?

It combines with order discounts and shipping discounts. Like any product discount, it doesn't stack with another product discount on the same item.

What data does Vendtier store?

Only your brands and each customer's brand percentages, stored in your own Shopify store. It doesn't read customer names, emails, phone numbers or addresses. See the privacy policy.
